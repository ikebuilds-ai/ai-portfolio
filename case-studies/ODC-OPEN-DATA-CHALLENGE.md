# Gold Coast Innovation Hub Open Data Challenge — Disaster Resilience

**Recognition for Rubik Resilience: recognised at the Gold Coast Innovation Hub Open Data Challenge (disaster resilience), pitched live on stage.**

## Honest framing — please read

The hosts **never publicly announced formal rankings or a winner**. So I describe this as **"recognised"** and **"pitched live on stage"** — not as "1st place" or "winner." A council disaster-management judge from Gold Coast City Council voted for the entry. This is true, and it's how I present it: recognised and judged as worthy, without overclaiming a ranking that was never announced.

## What happened

- Built Rubik Resilience (suburb-level disaster risk with an evidence ladder) around the **"Data Presents. Human Decides."** philosophy.
- **Pitched live on stage** at the Challenge.
- A **Gold Coast City Council disaster-management judge voted for the entry**.
- Photographed on-stage with a **Gold Coast City Council representative**.
