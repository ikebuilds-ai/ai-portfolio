# Credentials AI — Commercial Lead-Generation AI Product

**A separate, revenue-oriented product.** Free AI Profile → verified lead engine → paid, with **Stripe checkout live**. This is end-to-end product building: front end, backend, payments, deployment, and putting it in front of customers.

## What it is

- **Free AI-search profile** for a local business — a verified, single-source-of-truth presence AI search can surface.
- **Verified lead engine** — captures interest and turns it into **tracked enquiries**.
- **Paid tier via Stripe** — a real monetised funnel, not a hobby project.
- Live at **credentialsai.com.au** (company: erosium.com.au).

## Engineering depth

- **Real payments** — Stripe checkout wired and live; shows I can ship something people pay for.
- **Scanner-safe auth** — magic-link flow that first redirects through a confirmation step so automated email scanners can't silently redeem a session.
- **Verified lead funnel** — free profile → verification → tracked enquiry → conversion, with the dashboard showing live leads.
- **First-party transactional email lane** — owns its own server email (dashboard access, order notifications, lead alerts) rather than depending on a third party.

## The idea

**Data Presents. Human Decides.** It presents verified profiles and tracked enquiries; a human founder decides the next move. It's built to make a real business decision easy and honest.
