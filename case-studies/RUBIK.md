# Rubik Resilience — Disaster-Risk Evidence System

**Suburb-level disaster-risk scoring** built on an "evidence ladder" — every conclusion is backed by sources you can climb, not a black box.

## What it is

- **Suburb risk scoring** — assesses disaster resilience at a community level.
- **Evidence ladder** — every claim is traceable to a source (drawn from government data), so a reader can verify each link in the chain.
- **MONITOR / INVESTIGATE / ESCALATE** — clear, actionable triage rather than a vague score.

## Engineering depth

- **Multi-source grounding** — pulls from government sources and structures it into a usable risk view.
- **Auditable reasoning** — the evidence ladder turns an AI conclusion into something a human can inspect, challenge, and trust.
- **Decision-gated** — the output is a recommended action (monitor / investigate / escalate), not an automatic decision.

## The idea

**Data Presents. Human Decides.** Rubik presents every risk score with an evidence ladder a person can climb; a human reads it and decides what to do. No black boxes.
